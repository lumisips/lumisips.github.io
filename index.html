Current Development Phase

Advanced Product Development

LumiSips has progressed beyond early concept development and small experimental batches. Cancer ♋ Blue Dragon Flower has advanced through repeated flavor balancing, sweetness and acidity refinement, concentrate development, functional ingredient testing, natural color development, carbonation improvement, and initial bulk-batch scaling.

The brand is now moving through ingredient stability evaluation, preservation-system selection, packaging development, supplier validation, nutritional analysis, shelf-life testing, and commercial-readiness planning.

Founder Story

Built From The Lab Up

LumiSips began as a founder-led beverage development journey focused on creating premium hydration and energy products with real flavor, purposeful functionality, gemstone-inspired visual identity, and zodiac culture.

Instead of beginning with a finished private-label formula, LumiSips is being built through hands-on experimentation, supplier relationships, repeated formulation, documentation, community feedback, and a commitment to developing a product worthy of commercial production.
