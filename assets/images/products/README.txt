PRODUCT IMAGE STANDARD

All primary LumiSips bottle renders should use:

16 oz clear glass beverage bottle

Realistic crown cap

Official LumiSips logo

Accurate zodiac identity

Accurate gemstone-inspired liquid color

Premium beverage-label proportions

Realistic liquid transparency

Realistic glass reflections

Subtle condensation

Commercial studio lighting

Transparent background when possible

Vertical product composition

High-resolution source image

Preferred format:
WebP

Recommended source height:
1200 to 2000 pixels


DO NOT USE:

Generic water bottle shapes

Cartoon bottles

Fake brand logos

Stock beverage packaging

Unrealistic container proportions

Low-resolution images

Heavy backgrounds baked into the product render
